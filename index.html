03 / GOOD TO KNOW

A FEW DETAILS.
ALL CLEAR.

Before the first conversation.

Do I need an account to make a request?+

No. Browse the collection and send a request as a guest. An account lets you track requests you submit while signed in; earlier guest requests are not linked automatically.

Will I pay when I submit a request?+

No payment is taken online. Your request is an expression of interest. The studio confirms stock, service scope, appointment details and final pricing with you directly.

Are the prices shown final?+

Service prices are starting estimates. Items marked “On request” are quoted for your vehicle. Taxes and the final scope are confirmed by the studio before you commit.

How do I choose the right service?+

Use the care finder to explore options for your goal, or compare up to three services from the collection. Add your vehicle and any concerns to your request so the studio can discuss a suitable plan.

Can I request a preferred appointment date?+

Yes. Include your preferred date in the request notes. It is a preference, not a confirmed booking; the team will contact you to agree availability and next steps.
